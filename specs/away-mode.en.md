# Away Mode (Away Mode / OA relay)

**语言 / Language:** [中文](away-mode.md) · [English](away-mode.en.md)

When the Owner is not in front of the terminal, the captain's reports and escalations move to the IM relay session until the Owner declares they are back. The purpose: the Owner can still rule from a phone, and the captain does not stall waiting.

## 1. Entering and leaving

- Entering: the Owner says, in IM or in the terminal, "turn on away mode / I'm not at the computer". The captain replies with one confirmation line and writes "away started HH:MM" into the ledger.
- Leaving: the Owner says "I'm back". The captain gives one consolidated terminal summary of the rulings, actions and outstanding questions from the away period.
- If the Owner says "you don't need to watch this window" while away, it means they are not reading that session; the captain still reports as usual but does not wait for a reply.

## 2. Channels

- Escalation: **double-written** — the decision panel `ask add` (landing in the inbox) + one IM brief (the question + A/B/C + a recommendation, same numbering). Whichever side the Owner answers on, it lands in the same inbox.
- Reporting: send only results and things they must decide; information-level entries go into the ledger, not into IM.
- On the stakeholder (employee) side: while away, authority over the outward-facing line contracts — acknowledge an employee's message within seconds with "got it", then clear the substantive answer with the Owner over IM, and only reply once approved.

## 3. The scope of autonomous decisions while away

- The same as when present: three kinds escalate (irreversible production surfaces / business facts only a human knows / outward-facing publication), everything else decide autonomously + file a note + keep it reversible.
- If the Owner grants several items in one go before leaving (e.g. "1 deploy, 2B, 3A…"), execute them one by one by number and report back one by one; do not broaden the interpretation. "Anything you can handle yourself doesn't need me" = the autonomous scope widens to the items listed on that occasion, and still excludes the red lines.
- Deploying while away: the runbook must already exist, the backup must already be done, the rollback is one sentence; when it is done, use "the next business heartbeat succeeds" as the finishing criterion before reporting.

## 4. Monitors

- Mount in addition: polling of the Owner's direct-message conversation (cursor continuation, human messages only); panel answers landing in the inbox.
- The dangerous side of business health is pushed to IM as well; the channel guard keeps the IM fallback path available.
- When away mode ends: stop the direct-message polling, everything else as usual.

## 5. Records

- The ledger, line by line: time, item, which channel it went through, the Owner's verbatim answer (with its number), the execution result.
- Every ruling the Owner makes while away is a "precedent"; it goes onto the decision list to be cited later.
